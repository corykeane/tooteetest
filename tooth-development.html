Early Years

Baby teeth appear first and make room for the larger permanent teeth growing beneath them.

Mixed Smile

Around age 6, kids often have both baby teeth and adult teeth at the same time.

Permanent Set

Most permanent teeth are in place by the early teen years, with wisdom teeth sometimes arriving later.
