Count Your Teeth

Use a mirror with a grown-up and count how many teeth you can see. Baby teeth and adult teeth can be different sizes.

Practice Brushing

Brush gently for two minutes, reaching the front, back, and chewing surfaces of every tooth.

Ask Questions

Next time you visit the dentist, ask what each tool does and how your teeth are growing.
