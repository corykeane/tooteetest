--- layout: default title: For Parents tag: For Parents headline: Support Healthy Tooth Habits intro: Quick guidance for helping children understand their teeth, build routines, and feel prepared for dental visits. ---

Make It Routine

Brush morning and night, floss where teeth touch, and keep supplies easy for kids to reach with supervision.

Explain Changes

Loose baby teeth and new adult teeth are normal milestones. Simple explanations can make the process less stressful.

Partner With Pros

Regular dental checkups help catch problems early and confirm that teeth are coming in as expected.
