--- layout: default title: Tooth Anatomy tag: Tooth Anatomy headline: Inside a Tooth intro: Every tooth has layers that work together to protect nerves, handle chewing, and stay anchored in place. ---

Enamel

The hard outside layer that protects the tooth.

Dentin

The layer beneath enamel that makes up much of the tooth.

Pulp

The living center with nerves and blood vessels.

Root Support

Cementum and the periodontal ligament help hold the tooth in the jaw.
