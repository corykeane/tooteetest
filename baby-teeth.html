Why They Matter

Healthy baby teeth guide adult teeth into place and help jaw muscles practice chewing.

When They Arrive

The first baby tooth often appears around 6 months, and the full set is usually in by age 2 or 3.

How To Care

Brush gently every day and ask a dentist about the right fluoride toothpaste amount for your child.
