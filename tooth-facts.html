Baby Teeth

Most children grow 20 baby teeth. They save space for adult teeth and help kids practice chewing and speaking.

Baby teeth and tooth fairy tradition

Adult Teeth

Most adults have up to 32 permanent teeth, including wisdom teeth. These teeth are designed to last a lifetime with good care.

See the development chart

Strong Enamel

Enamel is the hard outer covering of each tooth. It protects the softer layers inside, but it still needs brushing and healthy foods.

Explore support tissues

Plaque Control

Plaque forms daily and needs regular brushing, flossing, rinsing, and dental visits to protect teeth and gums.
